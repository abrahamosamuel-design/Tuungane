## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust community marketplace and professional social connection platform tailored for economic empowerment across East Africa. It treats a person and the services they offer as distinct entities: bridging the structured, bank-grade reliability of professional platforms with the warm, vibrant immediacy of a hyper-local peer marketplace.

### Aesthetic Foundation & Tone
The design language adopts a **Corporate / Modern High-Contrast** aesthetic with tactile clarity:
- **60-30-10 Balanced Presence:** A dominant 60% deep institutional navy foundation delivering security, permanence, and regulatory stature; 30% electric sun orange driving commercial momentum, direct action, and discovery; and 10% growth green reserved for verified credentials, live availability, and positive assurance.
- **Outdoor & High-Glare Legibility:** Tailored specifically for varied mobile hardware and direct equatorial daylight. The interface eschews washed-out pastels or low-contrast opacity tricks in favor of crisp white card surfaces, hairline framing borders, and high-contrast charcoal-to-slate copy.
- **Dual Identity Expression:** The UI maintains distinct architectural visual expressions for personal identity (who you are: reputation, connections, human verification) versus commercial offerings (what you deliver: pricing models, turnaround times, and service execution).

## Colors

The color architecture is built around three distinct communicative imperatives: authority, kinetic conversion, and guaranteed trust.

### Role Allocations
- **Primary Navy (`#0B1F3A` / `#0B192C`):** Institutional anchor representing bank-grade governance, enterprise stability, and civic dignity. Applied to top navigation bars, authoritative headlines, structural framing, and primary system buttons.
- **Action Orange (`#FF6B1A`):** The catalytic conversion engine. Applied intentionally to actionable triggers—"Request Service", "Post a Request", Floating Action Buttons (FAB), active tab indicators, and rating star fills. Never used purely as background filler.
- **Growth & Verified Green (`#22A652`):** Communicates validated capability, live availability, and safety. Used strictly for "Verified Provider" badges, active beacon pings ("Available Now"), positive transaction notices, and background badge washes (`#E8F7EE`).
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas sits between pure `#FFFFFF` and serene tinted `#F8FAFC`.
  - Secondary containers and pill backgrounds leverage `#F1F5F9` and `#F0F2F5`.
  - Borders rely on crisp hairline boundaries (`#DADDE1` / `#E2E8F0`) rather than drop shadows alone to guarantee structure on mobile LCDs.
  - Body text uses `#050505` on desktop and outdoor-calibrated `#4B4F56` for secondary descriptions to eliminate eye strain under heavy lighting.

### Hero & Elevation Gradients
- **Oceanic Authority Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #0B192C 0%, #0B1F3A 60%, #1E3A8A 100%)` for hero backdrops and promotional headers.
- **Momentum Orange Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF6B1A 0%, #FF8A42 100%)` for conversion banners and spotlight achievements.

## Typography

The typographic system relies on **Inter** (and its native OS system fallsbacks: San Francisco, Segoe UI, Roboto) to ensure zero layout shift and instantaneous offline caching.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tight Modern Letter-Spacing:** All display and headline levels enforce a `-0.02em` tracking rule (`letter-spacing: -0.02em`), imparting an editorial, authoritative tone that prevents long titles from breaking uncomfortably.
- **Mobile-First Legibility Lift:** On screens smaller than 640px, base body copy is calibrated to `16.5px` and secondary copy is lifted to `15.5px`. Micro-copy (`caption-mobile`) is raised to `13.4px` with a generous `21.6px` line height to ensure readability in high-glare environments.
- **Compact Badge Normalization:** An explicit exception to mobile scaling: UI categorization pills ("Near you", "Flexible", "Urgent", "Work update") are normalized globally to `9px` bold uppercase text with positive tracking (`0.025em`). This keeps status indicators information-dense without occupying layout width.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is a mobile-first responsive grid system designed to support seamless transitions from single-thumb mobile operation to dense desktop dashboard utilities.

### Spacing Scale & Baseline
- **Unit Grid:** Built upon an 8pt modular rhythm (with 4pt half-steps for micro-alignments).
- **Component Padding Scale:**
  - `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between badge icons and labels.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text separation and compact list padding.
  - `space-md` (16px): Standard inner card padding and input guttering.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Directory card grids, structural separators, and container headers.
  - `space-xl` (32px): Major card margins and section breaks.

### Layout Breakpoints & Columns
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column vertical stream. Canvas margins shrink to `0.75rem` (`12px`) and section gutters drop to `0.5rem` (`8px`) to dedicate maximum horizontal width to card content, while preserving generous internal padding within cards (`16px` – `20px`).
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 2-column balanced grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column system constrained to a `max-w-7xl` (1280px) master container. Service directories form 3-column card layouts, while social/feed views employ an asymmetrical 3-column arrangement: 280px left navigation, 640px feed container, and 320px right contextual discovery column.
- **Physical Touch Target Minimum:** Every interactive tap zone enforces a hard minimum height of `44px` on viewports under 640px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines **hairline structural borders** with **tactile multi-layered ambient shadows**. This prevents interface elements from blending into each other while avoiding dark, heavy drops.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Surfaces sit flush on `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`. Dividers rely strictly on `1px solid #DADDE1`.
- **Level 1 (Resting Card / Directory Unit):** Used for marketplace cards, profile summaries, and feed tiles.
  - Border: `1px solid #DADDE1` (or `#E2E8F0`).
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.02)`.
  - Surface: Solid `#FFFFFF`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Lift):** Cards respond tactically to pointer interaction.
  - Transform: `translateY(-2px)`.
  - Shadow: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Elements & Bottom Navigation):** Floating Action Buttons (FAB), contextual sheets, and fixed navigation bars.
  - Bottom Bar: `#FFFFFF` at 95% opacity with `backdrop-blur-xl` and `border-t: 1px solid rgba(218, 221, 225, 0.8)`.
  - Center Action FAB: Elevated 56px circular button with `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.35)` and a 4px white buffer isolation ring.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Bottom Drawers):** Backdrop dim using `rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.60)` accompanied by `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a geometric yet approachable corner language calibrated at **Level 2 (Rounded)**, featuring progressive radius softening across screen breakpoints.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (`rounded-md` / `0.5rem` / 8px):** Checkboxes, form tags, and micro-badges.
- **Interactive Inputs & Primary Buttons (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / 12px):** Standard text inputs, dropdown triggers, and operational buttons. Provides an ergonomic tap profile.
- **Cards & Primary Modules (`rounded-2xl` / `1rem` / 16px):** Service directory cards, modal sheets, and personal bio cards. On mobile viewports (< 640px), these gracefully scale down to `0.875rem` (14px) to preserve screen real estate.
- **Interactive Action Elements (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status badges, proximity indicator chips, search bars, and conversion pills ("Request Service").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion Button (Orange):**
  - Geometry: `rounded-xl` or `rounded-full` for hero callouts; min-height `44px` on mobile, `40px` to `48px` on desktop.
  - Visual: Background `#FF6B1A`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `600`, shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(255, 107, 26, 0.28)`.
  - States: Hover `brightness-105 scale-[1.01]`, Active `scale-[0.98]`.
- **Primary Institutional Button (Navy):**
  - Geometry: `rounded-xl`; background `#0B1F3A`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `600`.
  - States: Hover `bg-[#0B192C]`, Active `scale-[0.98]`.
- **Secondary / Neutral Button:**
  - Geometry: `rounded-xl`; background `#F1F5F9`, foreground `#0B1F3A`, border `1px solid transparent`.
  - States: Hover `bg-[#FF6B1A]/10 text-[#FF6B1A] border-[#FF6B1A]/20`.
- **Ghost / Outline Button:**
  - Geometry: `rounded-xl`; background transparent, border `1px solid #DADDE1`, foreground `#0B1F3A`.

### Service Profile Card ("What You Offer" — Primary Marketplace Unit)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` card, `rounded-2xl`, border `1px solid #DADDE1`, resting elevation Level 1.
- **Top Bar:** Provider avatar (48px – 56px) with an orange or green ring; provider name in Navy SemiBold (`headline-sm`); category label in `#64748B`; verified checkmark badge (`#22A652`).
- **Rating Cluster:** 5 filled stars (`#FF6B1A`) with numeric score and total review counter; inline district/location pin.
- **Bottom Shelf:** Visually distinct shelf separated by a hairline top border, filled with `#F8FAFC`. Houses the real-time availability beacon (`#22A652` dot with "Available now") on the left and an orange text action ("View profile →") on the right.

### Personal Profile Container ("Who You Are")
- **Container:** Generous `#FFFFFF` card featuring a prominent circular avatar (64px – 80px), identity verification badge, full personal name, location subtitle, short bio, and dual action triggers ("Connect" in Navy, "Message" in Outline).
- **Service Stack Attachment:** Renders child service chips underneath the bio, explicitly linking personal identity to commercial capabilities.

### Badges & Status Pills
- **Global Normalization Rule:** Micro-badges strictly adhere to `9px` uppercase bold text, tracking `0.025em`, padding `1px 6px`, and `10px` inline icons.
- **Verified Badge:** Background `#E8F7EE`, border `1px solid #A3E2B8`, text `#22A652`. Accompanied by a checkmark icon.
- **Opportunity / Urgent Badge:** Background `#FF6B1A`/10, text `#FF6B1A`, border `1px solid #FF6B1A`/20.
- **Proximity Badge ("Near you"):** Mint/Green background pill displaying a location pin and calculated distance in kilometers.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search Hero Input:** Floating `rounded-full` container, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DADDE1`, padding `14px 20px`. Focus states introduce a 2px outer ring in `#FF6B1A` at 20% opacity with border `#FF6B1A`.
- **Standard Input & Textarea:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DADDE1`, corner radius `rounded-xl`, min-height `44px`. Navy semibold labels positioned above fields with `space-xs` clearance.

### Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar with FAB
- **Container:** Fixed bottom dock (`height: 64px`), background `#FFFFFF` (95% opacity with `backdrop-blur-xl`), border-top `1px solid #DADDE1`, safe-area bottom padding.
- **5-Column Array:** Home, Services, Center Elevated Action, Opportunities, Profile. Active tabs illuminate in `#FF6B1A`; inactive tabs remain in `#64748B`.
- **Center Elevated FAB:** Protruding 56px circular button (`#FF6B1A`) with a white plus icon, elevated `-24px` above the navigation boundary, protected by a 4px white outer ring and orange shadow. Triggers the primary creation action ("List a Service" vs. "Post a Request").
